# Testing Lucid

Thanks for helping us test **Lucid**, an app that renames lots of files at once, safely.
You don't need any technical knowledge. This takes about 15 minutes.

**Your files are safe.** Lucid shows you every change before it happens, never overwrites a
file, and can put names back the way they were at any time. It also works completely
offline: nothing is ever uploaded.

## 1. Open Lucid

**Windows:** double-click **Lucid.exe**.
If a blue box says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
(This appears because the prototype isn't registered with Microsoft yet.)

**Mac:** double-click the zip to unpack it, then drag **Lucid** into your Applications folder.
The first time, **right-click** (or Control-click) Lucid and choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
(Macs ask this for apps that aren't registered with Apple yet. You only need to do it once.)

**Linux:** unpack the download, then double-click **Lucid**. If nothing happens, right-click it
and choose **Run as a Program**, or open **Properties → Permissions** and tick
**Allow executing file as program**. After the first time, Lucid also appears in your app menu
with its logo, so you can open it from there or pin it to your dock.

It can take a few seconds to open the first time.

## 2. Try these tasks

Please use the **sample files** first: click **"New here? Try it with sample files"**.
Lucid makes a practice folder in your Documents folder, so nothing of yours is touched.

1. **Number some files.** Click *Number my files* on the left, then **Rename**.
2. **Put it back.** Click **Restore original names** in the purple message.
3. **Name the photos by the date they were taken.** Select just the photos in the list
   (hold Ctrl, or ⌘ on a Mac, and click each one), then choose *Name photos by date taken*.
4. **Fix a clash.** Add the rule *Build a new name* and type `Report` as the new name.
   Lucid will warn you that names clash. Use the button it offers to fix it.
5. **Rename one file by hand.** Double-click a name in the list and type a new one.
6. **Your own files (optional).** Drag in a folder you'd like to tidy and try anything you like.
   **History** (top right) lets you undo any rename later, even after closing Lucid.
